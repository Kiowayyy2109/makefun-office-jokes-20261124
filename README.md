Entry for the make.fun bounty **Make the AI Judge Laugh: Office Jokes**.

`main.py` writes a single one-line joke about office life to
`/output/entry.txt`:

> We replaced the suggestion box with a shredder, and for the first time
> ever, management responded to every suggestion within seconds.

The line was chosen from a field of candidates against the judge's
published brief: a genuine joke (not a slogan or an observation), a real
turn at the end, the recognisable truth of the office in it, tight
wording, and a fresh take. Before writing, the program validates the
text against the competition gate: one line, 1 to 199 characters, not
only whitespace.

Run it with:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
